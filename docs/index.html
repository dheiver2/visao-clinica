Features

What the software delivers

Webcam capture

OpenCV + MediaPipe extract tremor, micro-expressions, eye tracking, facial symmetry and body movement.

Native local AI

BitNet b1.58 2B4T runs on your own machine, with no external APIs, to correlate findings and generate reports.

Instant results

Deterministic clinical indicators in milliseconds; the AI narrative is optional and runs in the background.

Controlled accuracy

Each indicator reports a confidence; only medium/high-accuracy ones are shown. A poor capture triggers a warning.

PDF & CSV reports

Technical export, local SQLite persistence, and Research and Screening modes.

Privacy by design

No data leaves the computer. No external services — ideal for medical confidentiality.

Scientific basis

Techniques validated in high-impact literature

rPPG — contactless vital signs

Heart rate and variability (HRV) from skin color. Wang et al., IEEE TBME 2017; Poh et al., Optics Express 2010.

Oculomotor metrics

Fixation stability (BCEA) and saccade main sequence. Anderson & MacAskill, Nat. Rev. Neurol. 2013.

Facial hypomimia (FACS)

Reduced Action Unit dynamics. Bandini et al., J. Neurosci. Methods 2017; Ekman FACS.

Motor stereotypies

Repetitive movement via autocorrelation. Goodwin et al., J. Autism Dev. Disord. 2011.

Scientific notice and limitations. Screening and research-support tool. Results are not a medical diagnosis and do not replace evaluation by healthcare professionals.
  • Indicator thresholds are heuristic and literature-based, but have not yet been calibrated on a dedicated clinical dataset.
  • Down syndrome and ASD signs require specialist evaluation (craniofacial morphology, genetics, behavioral assessment) — the indicator is only an aid.
  • Accuracy varies with lighting, camera quality and capture length; the app reports a confidence level per indicator and avoids showing low-confidence results.
  • No data leaves the user's device — 100% local processing (privacy details).
